Riska N. Auliani

📍 Semarang, Central Java 🔬 PADI Divemaster & Scientific Diver

Riska N. Auliani is a PADI Divemaster and Scientific Diver from Semarang, Central Java, Indonesia, passionate about marine conservation and sustainable ecotourism. As a recipient of the Coral Catch Scholarship and the Abli Aqua Divemaster programme, she has trained in both professional diving and hands-on reef monitoring.

Her work focuses on connecting responsible tourism with genuine conservation outcomes — making sure that divers and visitors understand the impact they have on coral reefs and coastal communities.

Use Riska’s story to talk about careers in marine science, women in field research, and how tourism can be designed to support, not damage, marine ecosystems.
