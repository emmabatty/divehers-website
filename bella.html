Story 05

Isabelle Batty Sukerta

📍 Canada 🌊 Divemaster & Marine Conservation Advocate

Isabelle went on to complete a Divemaster internship and scholarship in Raja Ampat, where she spent four months developing her diving and leadership skills. Following this experience, she moved to Canada, where she is now pursuing a degree in Biomedical Science.

Isabelle continues to build on what she learned from the Coral Catch program, integrating her passion for marine conservation into her studies and sharing her knowledge to inspire ocean awareness within her new community. Her journey showcases how diving experiences can shape a lifelong commitment to conservation, even as careers and locations change.

Use Isabelle's story to explore how global experiences in marine conservation can influence academic paths and inspire international ocean advocacy.
