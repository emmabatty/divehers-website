Kanya Anindya Maharani Wijaya

📍 Indonesia 🪸 Marine Science & Coral Conservation Advocate

Kanya Wijaya is a passionate marine science and coral conservation advocate from Indonesia. She was part of Coral Catch 2024, where she gained hands-on experience in coral restoration and reef protection. An alumna of the SUSI Global Student Leaders 2023 program, Kanya combines scientific knowledge with community engagement to promote sustainable marine ecosystems.

Her work with organizations like Yayasan Gili Matra and collaboration with Ghent University highlights her commitment to bridging research, conservation, and local action. Through her efforts, she demonstrates how combining scientific expertise with grassroots engagement can drive meaningful change for our oceans.

Use Kanya's story to inspire conversations about marine science, coral conservation, and the power of combining research with community action to protect our reefs.
