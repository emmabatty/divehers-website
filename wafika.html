Wafika Amira

📍 Bandung, Indonesia 🌱 Duta Mangrove Indonesia 2024

Wafika Amira Nurfitri is a passionate marine conservationist and environmental advocate from Bandung, Indonesia. As Duta Mangrove Indonesia 2024, she leads efforts to promote mangrove restoration, community engagement, and environmental education through initiatives like Mangrove Goes to School.

A student of Marine and Fisheries Education at Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia, Wafika combines scientific knowledge with strong leadership in sustainability outreach.

In 2025, she was awarded the Blue Corner Dive PADI Women’s Day Scholarship, recognizing her dedication to empowering women in diving and ocean conservation. Her work continues to inspire youth across Indonesia to protect coastal ecosystems and climate-vulnerable communities.
