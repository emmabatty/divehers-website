Natasya Caterina

📍 Indonesia 🌊 PADI Divemaster

Natasya Caterina is a passionate diver and ocean advocate whose journey began with a simple love for the sea. Over time, that passion grew into a commitment to make underwater spaces safer and more welcoming for women and girls.

Through her work, she shows that you don’t have to fit a certain stereotype to belong in the dive community — you just have to care, learn, and keep showing up.

Use Natasya’s story to open conversations about confidence, community, and taking up space in traditionally male-dominated environments.
