Story 07

Dilla

📍 Indonesia 🔬 Marine Researcher & Coral Conservation Specialist

Dilla is a Biology graduate from Hasanuddin University with a strong passion for marine research and coral reef conservation. During her time with Coral Catch, one of her favorite activities was participating in the Ecological Monitoring Program (EMP), particularly conducting Line Intercept Transect (LIT) surveys.

She enjoys this method because it challenges her to stay focused underwater, think critically, and analyze data with precision — all while strengthening her buoyancy control. Through these experiences, Dilla continues to grow as a skilled diver and marine conservationist dedicated to protecting Indonesia's reefs.

Use Dilla's story to inspire conversations about scientific diving, marine research, and how rigorous ecological monitoring contributes to reef conservation efforts.
