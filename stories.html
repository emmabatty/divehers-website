Stories

DiveHer began as a short film celebrating Indonesia's first all-female coral restoration team. Since then, it has grown into a project that highlights Indonesian women in marine science, conservation, and diving through short, inspiring interviews. DiveHer now serves as a platform that shares their stories, showcases their impact, and encourages young Indonesian girls to see themselves in the ocean and in the science that protects it.
