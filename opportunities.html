Opportunities

Finding opportunities as a young person can be challenging, especially in the marine and environmental fields where information is often scattered or hard to access. This page is updated every month to make the search easier by sharing carefully curated opportunities for youth.

It includes internships, fellowships, scholarships, conferences, and other programs designed to support young people interested in ocean, climate, and environmental work. Whether you are looking to learn, develop skills, or grow your impact, this page is here to help you find the pathways that fit your goals.

Future Swell Creator Lab

Remote
Deadline: May 15, 2026

An 8-week accelerator empowering ocean conservation creators with storytelling, campaign, and leadership skills to drive meaningful change for planet ocean through mentorship, training, and creative projects.

Nat Geo & The Nature Conservancy – Marine & Community Conservation Externship

Remote
Deadline: May 17, 2026

A 6–8 week remote externship (June 1 – July 27) for ages 18–25 interested in marine and community conservation. No prior experience required. Includes workshops, speaker series, and a conservation project. $500 stipend available.

Save Our Seas Foundation – Keystone Grant

Global
Deadline: May 13, 2026 (Stage I, 18:00 CET)

A biennial research grant averaging $25,000 USD per year for projects focused on sharks, rays, skates, sawfishes, and chimaeras. Two-stage application process — Stage I is a short online form, Stage II by invitation only. Open in even years (2026, 2028, etc.).

Wild Wonder Foundation – Club Micro Grant

Global
Deadline: May 29, 2026

A micro grant supporting the establishment and growth of Nature Journaling Clubs around the world, with a focus on bringing nature connection to underserved communities. The Wild Wonder Foundation uses art, science, curiosity, and community to encourage conservation.

2026 Youth Innovation Challenge (YIC)

Global (award ceremony in Taiwan)
Deadline: May 31, 2026

A global challenge for ages 15–30 to submit innovative marine and ocean conservation solutions. Winners receive a $1,000 USD prize and a fully funded trip to Taiwan to present at an international forum.

Cape Town, South Africa (One Young World Summit, Nov 3–6)
Deadline: June 1, 2026

A fully funded scholarship for 10 young leaders (ages 18–30) working on climate solutions including marine ecosystem protection. Covers flights, accommodation, meals, and transport to the One Young World Summit in Cape Town, plus access to an exclusive pre-programme.

2026 Ocean Awareness Contest

Remote
Deadline: June 8, 2026

The 2026 Ocean Awareness Contest – Your Story, Our Ocean – invites you to explore how the ocean sustains, protects, and inspires us all. Share your personal connection to the ocean through art, writing, performance, film, or multimedia.

ICMM Young Leaders Scholarship 2026

Cape Town, South Africa (One Young World Summit, Nov 3–6)
Deadline: June 7, 2026

A fully funded scholarship for young leaders (ages 18–35) driving impact in biodiversity conservation, decarbonisation, or sustainability. Scholars attend the One Young World Summit in Cape Town and gain lifelong ambassador membership, mentorship, and leadership training.

Youth Empowerment Forum (YEF) 2026

Geneva, Switzerland (Sep 3–6, 2026)
Deadline: July 5, 2026

A fully funded global leadership forum gathering 250 young changemakers (ages 16–46) in Geneva for policy dialogues, leadership workshops, and networking with global participants. Open to all nationalities.

Indonesia (Online)

A free digital skills scholarship for Indonesian women by Perempuan Inovasi GENerasi AI in collaboration with IBM SkillsBuild. Covers AI, technology, and career readiness training. Open call: March 18 – May 30, 2026.

Leadership Certification in Global Diplomacy 2026 (HISA)
